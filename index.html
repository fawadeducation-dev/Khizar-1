Project Synopsis

Guardian AI is an AI-assisted wearable emergency detection system designed in the form of a smart locket. It combines a heart-rate sensor, motion sensor, camera, AI processor, GPS/GNSS, cellular communication, vibration motor, rechargeable battery and supplementary solar charging.

During normal operation, the system monitors sensor information using a low-power approach. When an unusual combination of signals is detected, such as a possible fall together with other warning signals, the AI processor enters a verification stage.

During verification, the camera can capture a short event recording and the GPS module can determine the approximate location. If the prototype's predefined emergency conditions are satisfied, the device vibrates to notify the wearer and sends an alert to an authorized guardian through the cellular network.

This website is an interactive simulation of the proposed system. The displayed sensor readings, AI decisions and emergency events are simulated and are not readings from physical hardware.
